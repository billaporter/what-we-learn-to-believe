Progeny

2014
Acrylic and transferred ink on wood
7 x 7 inches

Visual Description

A small, square painting on a wooden panel depicts an American robin perched on a horizontal orange branch. The bird faces left, with a dark charcoal-gray head and back, a reddish-orange breast, a white lower belly, and a yellow beak. Its feathers are rendered in fine detail, with delicate white and gray lines defining the wings and long, dark tail. The background is divided diagonally into two large areas of color: a deep, rusty red in the upper-left portion and an off-white in the lower-right. Faint printed text, resembling pages from an old book, is visible beneath the paint in both sections. The orange branch stretches across the lower portion of the composition, with thick drips of paint hanging downward at irregular intervals. The painting combines a naturalistic illustration of the bird with bold geometric color fields, weathered textures, and expressive paint drips.
