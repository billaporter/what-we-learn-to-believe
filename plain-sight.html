Plain Sight

2026
Acrylic and mixed media on wood
36 x 36 inches

Visual Description

A large, nearly square painting combines flat, geometric shapes, cartoon imagery, and expressive layers of paint. The background is divided into two sections: pale lavender on the left and blue on the right, separated by a thick, angular black line. On the right, a cartoon-style figure with pink skin, a long pointed red nose, oversized white eyes, and prominent buck teeth wears a white cap and a short-sleeved white shirt. The figure leans forward with a wide, open-mouthed expression, framed within a large, black-bordered geometric shape resembling a screen or window. On the left, a second cartoon figure faces the first. Its body and face are heavily obscured by thick, gestural layers of yellow, pink, red, green, and white paint, with scratches, smears, and drips extending downward. Portions of the figure's pointed nose, red mouth, teeth, thin legs, and white shoes remain visible beneath the paint. Along the far-left edge, cropped geometric shapes in dark blue, cream, teal, and orange suggest fragments of another image or interior setting. A thick black vertical form with a curved base stands between the two figures, while a horizontal gray band runs along the bottom. The composition contrasts the clean outlines and flat colors of the cartoon figure on the right with the chaotic, heavily worked surface of the figure on the left, creating a visual tension between clarity and obscurity.
