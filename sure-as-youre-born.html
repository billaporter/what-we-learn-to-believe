Sure as You’re Born

2014
Acrylic and mixed media on reclaimed wood
10 x 18 ¾ inches

Visual Description

A vertically oriented mixed-media assemblage constructed from overlapping pieces of reclaimed wood of varying widths and lengths. The irregularly shaped panels extend beyond one another at the bottom, creating an uneven edge. The wood is heavily weathered, with visible grain, cracks, scratches, nail holes, and areas of chipped paint. The composition combines exposed brown wood with geometric fields of muted red, white, gray, and blue. Near the top, a large red section meets a white triangular shape along a sharp diagonal. Below it, a narrow gray band separates the white area from a larger field of distressed blue paint. A thin orange stripe runs diagonally across the composition, appearing on both the left and right sides, interrupted by the overlapping wooden panels. Near the lower-right portion, a partially obscured white animal figure resembling a rabbit appears against the dark blue background. Its head faces left, with one long, pointed ear extending upward. The figure is rendered in rough, textured brushstrokes, blending into the weathered surface. The assemblage combines geometric abstraction with fragments of recognizable imagery, emphasizing the physical qualities of reclaimed wood through its layered construction, irregular edges, and distressed surfaces.
