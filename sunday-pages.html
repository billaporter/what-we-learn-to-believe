Sunday Pages

2018
Acrylic and book pages on wood
38 x 40 inches

Visual Description

A large, vertically oriented painting constructed from overlapping rows of cedar wood shingles, arranged in a staggered, brick-like pattern. The surface is predominantly off-white, with visible wood grain, weathered textures, and faint printed text resembling pages from old books beneath the paint. Across the upper portion, a large cartoon-style storm cloud stretches nearly the full width of the composition. The cloud is outlined in black, with rounded, billowing forms and short diagonal lines suggesting shading. Thin yellow rays and black lines radiate outward from behind the cloud toward the top and side edges. A large, jagged yellow lightning bolt extends downward from the cloud on the left, ending in a sharp point near the middle of the painting. In the lower-right corner, a partially visible blue cartoon figure with a single large, white eye peers out from behind a rectangular yellow structure. The figure and structure are cropped by the right edge of the painting. A thin yellow drip extends from the bottom of the structure toward the gray band that runs across the bottom of the composition. The painting combines bold cartoon imagery, geometric forms, and the weathered texture of cedar shingles, contrasting the dramatic storm cloud and lightning with the small, partially concealed figure below.
