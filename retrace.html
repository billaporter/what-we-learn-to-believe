Retrace

2014
Acrylic and mixed media on wood
26 x 36 inches

Visual Description

A large, vertically oriented painting constructed from rectangular wooden panels arranged in five horizontal rows, with panels of varying widths creating a staggered, mosaic-like pattern. The surface combines exposed wood grain, weathered paint, and faint printed text beneath layers of muted red, rust, blue, yellow, cream, gray, and natural wood tones. The composition resembles a patchwork of fragmented images and abstract color fields. Small details appear throughout, including a white curved line against a red panel, partial black lettering, a bird's wing, a pixelated white cloud against blue, a red-framed window, and fragments of cartoon characters and illustrations. Other panels contain geometric shapes, drips of paint, small drawings, and sections of printed text resembling pages from old books. A horizontal row of small, dark circular marks runs across the upper portion of the painting, resembling nail holes. Several images are interrupted by the boundaries between panels, while others appear partially obscured beneath layers of paint. The composition combines geometric organization with scattered visual fragments, contrasting the orderly arrangement of wooden panels with the varied colors, textures, and imagery distributed across the surface.
