What We Hold 2026 Solid and composite wood table, glazed stoneware bowl, mustard seeds, speaker, and digital audio 28” x 18” x 18”

Visual Description: What We Hold is a multisensory installation featuring a wooden table, a ceramic bowl filled with mustard seeds, and a suspended light and speaker fixture. The work invites visitors to engage through touch, sound, and reflection. At the center of the installation is a small rectangular table constructed from solid and composite wood. Resting on its surface is a glazed stoneware bowl containing numerous tiny mustard seeds. Visitors are invited to touch and interact with the seeds. Suspended above the table is a combined light and speaker fixture. The light illuminates the bowl and tabletop, while the speaker plays a 36-minute, 38-second looping audio conversation between artist Bill Porter and poet Jeanetta “Outta Sight” Price. Their conversation explores blindness, faith, doubt, family, identity, disability, and the experiences that shape what people come to believe.
