Color Stories

2013
Mixed media on paint swatches
12 × 12 inches

Visual Description

A square composition consisting of 25 rectangular color swatches arranged in a grid of five rows and five columns, separated by narrow white spaces. Each swatch resembles a paint color sample, with a color name and numerical code printed near the bottom or along the left edge. The colors range from deep reds, oranges, and blues to pale grays, whites, and greens. Each swatch contains a small, whimsical illustration, combining cartoon imagery, familiar objects, and surreal elements. The drawings include faces, birds, a robot, a raised white-gloved hand, a small house, a ship inside a bottle, a flying dove, a sheep, and a glass of orange liquid. Some illustrations are rendered in black linework, while others incorporate color, shading, or sketch-like textures. The swatches carry names such as “burnt peanut red,” “adobe orange,” “smoke,” “seaside blue,” “electric blue,” “watermelon red,” “dark salmon,” “iceberg,” “little boy blue,” “true blue,” “shadow gray,” “misty memories,” “chalk white,” “american white,” “heaven,” “clearspring green,” “stem green,” “gray owl,” “peach sorbet,” “tangy orange,” “irish moss,” “douglas fir,” “camouflage,” “carrot stick,” and “orange.” The overall composition resembles a commercial paint color chart, with playful, sometimes absurd illustrations interrupting the orderly arrangement of labeled colors.
