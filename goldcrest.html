Goldcrest

2022
Acrylic on wood
24 x 36 inches

Visual Description

A vertically oriented painting constructed from overlapping rows of cedar wood shingles, arranged in a staggered, brick-like pattern. The weathered surface features exposed wood grain, faint printed text, and layers of gray, beige, and yellow paint, with long yellow drips running down from the upper edge. A bright blue vertical band extends from the top to the bottom of the painting, slightly right of center. Two pale blue and white pixelated cloud-like forms stretch horizontally across the upper portion, resembling stepped geometric shapes. On the left, a small gray-and-white bird with a yellow stripe on its head perches on a black, leafless tree branch that extends diagonally across the composition. Several fragments of black-and-white cartoon imagery appear along the blue band, partially obscured by the blue paint. Near the top, part of a white-gloved hand is visible. Farther down, a pointed, ear-like shape emerges from the left side of the band, while small black and white shapes appear near the bottom. The composition combines naturalistic bird imagery, fragmented cartoon figures, geometric forms, and expressive paint drips against the textured surface of cedar shingles.
