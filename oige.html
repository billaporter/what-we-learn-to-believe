Óige

2013
Acrylic and mixed media on wood
45 1/2 x 24 1/2 inches

Visual Description

A horizontal arrangement of 28 small, square paintings displayed in four evenly spaced rows of seven against a white background. Each painting is created on a separate wooden panel, with visible wood grain, weathered surfaces, and layered paint in muted shades of blue, red, brown, gray, and cream. The individual panels feature fragments of imagery, including birds, clouds, architectural details, cartoon characters, abstract shapes, and everyday objects. Some depict recognizable subjects, such as a white pixelated cloud against a blue sky, a black bird in flight, a red cartoon figure with long antennae, a white speech bubble, and a pair of red-framed windows. Others contain partial images, textured surfaces, or small sections of larger scenes that extend beyond the edges of the panels. The paintings combine flat areas of color, expressive brushwork, printed textures, and both cartoon-style and more naturalistic imagery. Several panels contain only minimal marks or subtle variations in color, while others feature bold graphic elements. The consistent size and orderly grid contrast with the varied imagery and weathered surfaces, creating a collection of small, seemingly disconnected visual fragments that invite viewers to consider relationships between the individual paintings.
