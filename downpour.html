Downpour

2022
Acrylic and book pages on reclaimed wood
38 x 48 inches

Visual Description

A large, nearly square painting on vertically arranged wooden planks with a gently arched top. The surface is a weathered beige, with visible wood grain, nail holes, and faint layers of printed text embedded throughout. A large white cartoon-style cloud, outlined in black with pale blue shading, stretches across the upper-left portion of the painting. Three vertical bands of bright red, pale green, and sky blue descend from the cloud to the bottom edge. Splashes and drips of red, green, and blue paint rise diagonally from the lower portion of the composition, contrasting with the more controlled vertical bands of color. On the right, a small black-and-white bird perches atop the chimney of a sharply angled house that extends beyond the painting's right and bottom edges. The house is rendered in black outlines, with its roof and siding filled with densely printed text resembling pages from a book. The composition combines cartoon imagery, geometric bands of color, expressive paint splatters, and the weathered texture of reclaimed wood.
