Partly Cloudy

2017
Acrylic and book pages on reclaimed wood
15 × 22 inches

Visual Description

A vertically oriented painting on a surface of narrow, vertically arranged wooden panels, painted pale blue. The wood grain, seams, and subtle brushstrokes remain visible beneath the paint. Near the upper-right corner, a white, pixelated cartoon cloud outlined in black is partially cropped by the top edge. Small patches of pale blue appear within the cloud, reinforcing its blocky, digital appearance. Slightly below the center, a small, isolated fragment resembling a bird’s feather or wing appears against the blue background. The fragment is rendered in black, gray, and white, with a thin white streak extending downward from one end. In the lower-left corner, a sharply angled section of a building extends into the composition, its roofline rising and falling in pointed peaks. The building is outlined in black, with diagonal lines suggesting siding and small dark windows. Its surface is filled with printed text resembling pages from a book. The composition combines pixelated cartoon imagery, a fragment of naturalistic illustration, and architectural elements against an expansive blue background.
