Visual Description

A vintage Sony Sports Walkman portable cassette player stands upright accompanied by a pair of lightweight headphones resting to its right. The Walkman has a bright yellow plastic casing with dark gray and black accents. The word “SONY” appears in black near the top, while the lower front panel features the word “SPORTS” in large orange letters above “WALKMAN” and “FM/AM” in smaller white lettering. A translucent gray cassette compartment reveals part of the cassette inside, with a handwritten label reading “Hip Hop.” A radio frequency scale and a small speaker grille occupy the right side of the device. The headphones have a thin gray headband and two circular earpieces covered in bright yellow foam. A long gray audio cable connects the headphones to the Walkman, looping loosely across the white surface between them. The work features a looping audio compilation of hip-hop lyrics that use the word “blind” and related references as metaphors, highlighting how popular language and music represent blindness.
