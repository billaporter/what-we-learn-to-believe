Traces

2014
Acrylic and transferred ink on wood
7 x 7 inches

Visual Description

A small, square painting on a wooden panel features a weathered, textured surface divided horizontally into two sections. The upper portion is off-white, with faint red-orange marks, scratches, and scuffs concentrated near the center. The lower portion is painted a muted blue-gray, with subtle traces of red showing through the paint. Near the bottom center, the back of Donald Duck’s head is partially visible. His distinctive dark blue sailor cap sits atop his head, with a small black tassel extending to the right. His white feathers fill the lower portion of the image, disappearing beyond the bottom edge of the painting. The composition combines flat areas of color, distressed textures, and a cropped cartoon character, leaving much of the surface open and emphasizing the contrast between the worn background and Donald Duck’s recognizable silhouette.
