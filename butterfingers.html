Butterfingers

2023
Acrylic on reclaimed wood
14½ × 22 inches

Visual Description

A painting on a vertically paneled wooden surface features a vivid orange-red background with a large, light blue cartoon-style cloud near the top. Two white, four-fingered cartoon gloves outlined in black extend downward from the cloud on long, narrow black arms. The hands are open, with fingers loosely curled, as though reaching for or attempting to grasp something. Below the hands, several thin, irregular black vertical lines extend toward the bottom of the painting, suggesting an object slipping or falling out of reach. The orange-red paint covers most of the wooden surface, leaving a narrow strip of exposed natural wood along the bottom edge. The vertical grooves, wood grain, and imperfections remain visible, emphasizing the reclaimed material.
