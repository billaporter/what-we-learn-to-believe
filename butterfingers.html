Butterfingers

2023
Acrylic on reclaimed wood
14½ × 22 inches

Visual Description

A painting on a vertically paneled wooden surface features a bright orange-red background with a large, light blue cloud near the top. Two white cartoon-style gloved hands, outlined in black, extend downward from the cloud on long, black arms. The hands reach toward one another but do not touch. Below them, several thin, vertical black lines extend toward the bottom of the painting, suggesting falling or downward motion. A narrow strip of exposed natural wood runs along the bottom edge, with traces of chipped paint and visible wear.
