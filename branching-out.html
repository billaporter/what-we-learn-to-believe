Branching Out

2021
Acrylic, graphite, and book pages on wood
24 × 36 inches

Visual Description

A vertically oriented painting on a surface constructed from overlapping rows of cedar wood shingles, creating a staggered, brick-like pattern. The shingles are painted predominantly off-white, with faint traces of color, visible wood grain, and dark seams separating them. On the left, a tall, black, leafless tree rises from a gray ground into a small rectangular patch of blue sky. Three black birds appear in flight near its upper branches. A large white cartoon-style cloud, outlined in black, extends into the composition from the upper-right edge, partially obscuring a cropped figure. In the lower-right corner, a bright red cartoon bird with a white chest, yellow beak, and blue patterned cap stands against the gray ground. A long shadow stretches from the tree toward the bird. The composition combines the weathered texture and geometric arrangement of cedar shingles with flat, brightly colored cartoon imagery.
