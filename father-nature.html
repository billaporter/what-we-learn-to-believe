Father Nature

2018
Acrylic and book pages on wood
30 x 40 inches

Visual Description

A large, vertically oriented painting constructed from overlapping rows of cedar wood shingles, arranged in a staggered, brick-like pattern. The surface is painted pale blue, with visible brushstrokes, wood grain, and dark seams separating the shingles. In the upper-right corner, a large white cartoon-style cloud outlined in black fills much of the sky. Bright yellow rays extend outward from behind the cloud toward the top and left edges of the painting. Thin black lines radiate across the blue background, emphasizing the burst of light. Despite the sunshine, long streams of white and pale blue paint drip downward from the cloud, suggesting heavy rainfall. The thick, textured drips extend through much of the right half of the composition, contrasting with the flat colors and clean outlines of the cartoon imagery. In the lower-left corner, the back of a cartoon child's head and shoulder is partially visible, cropped by the edges of the painting. The child has bright yellow hair, a pale ear, and a red shirt, and appears to be looking toward the cloud.
